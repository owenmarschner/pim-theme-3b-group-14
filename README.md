# Theme 3B: Mathematical Exploration Project

Group project for **MATH30024 — Perspectives in Mathematics**, worth **50% of the unit**.

## Task

Choose and independently study a topic in mathematics or mathematics education beyond the standard curriculum. The mathematical content should be at third-year level and unfamiliar from our previous university studies. Use scholarly sources, such as textbooks and peer-reviewed papers, to develop a clear, well-supported explanation demonstrating our understanding. The brief does not require original research findings.

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Requirements | Weight | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group report | Aim for 20–30 pages, with a maximum of 30. Submit through Blackboard, including any supporting code or additional material. | 25% | 27 November 2026, 12 noon |
| Group presentation | A 15-minute presentation, assessed by lecturers and peers. | 15% | 3 December 2026 |
| Individual reflections and AI-use statements | Include at the end of the report, signed by each group member. | 10% | With the report |

We must also complete peer assessment of group members’ contributions; the assessment information lists a deadline of **4 December 2026, 12 noon**.

## Preparation and assessment

- Settle the topic in Week 4 and submit a draft report in Week 8 for feedback.
- Give a practice presentation in Week 10.
- Produce a coherent report in a consistent voice, with accurate mathematical typesetting and referencing.
- Demonstrate mathematical understanding, interpretation, clear communication and effective teamwork.
- Ask questions and provide feedback during other groups’ presentations.

Code, simulations, visualisations, posters or videos can help the project stand out, but are **optional**.

## AI use

The assessment permits AI for preliminary concept exploration, initial literature research, and spelling or grammar corrections. Substantiate ideas using scholarly sources and read suggested references ourselves. AI should not be cited as a source or used for substantial rewrites. Each member must declare their use.
